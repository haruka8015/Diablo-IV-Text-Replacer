The Horadric Cube makes a return from Diablo II with the Lord of Hatred expansion, a legendary tool used for all manner of crafting and item modifications.

The Horadric Cube can be used to modify many different categories of items in Diablo IV. The core of this feature is using recipes and rare crafting materials to make alterations to one or several items, with the main crafting material being different variants of "Primordial Dust" and "Tuning Prisms". The Horadric Cube becomes available by playing through the Lord of Hatred campaign and is located in Temis afterwards.

How it works

  • Utilize the 3-to-1recipe.
    • You can use this to collect rare items that cannot be target farmable, such as Tibault's Will.
    • Use it on the bricked items you get while farming for the big transfiguration outcomes, such as [x]Damage Type + modifiable, so that the bricked items are not wasted.
